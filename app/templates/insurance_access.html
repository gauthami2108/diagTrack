What the levels mean
  • None: no Insurance screen.
  • View, own centres: sees policies for the centres they belong to, and ones covering every centre; cannot change anything.
  • View, all centres: sees every policy; cannot change anything.
  • Manage: sees every policy, adds, edits, renews, archives and restores, and sees Activity.
  • Money figures (sum insured, premium) are shown to administrators, Manage, and anyone in the Management role who has any access, unless switched off in Settings.
  • Administrator: everything, plus this page, Cover types, and deleting an archived record for good.
