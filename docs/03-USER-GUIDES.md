# Staff guide

For everyone who uses DiagTrack: staff who report problems, agents and department heads who fix them, and managers
who oversee them. Print the sections that apply to each person.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open DiagTrack in the browser at the address your administrator gives you.
2. Type your user name and password, then press **Sign in**.
3. On a phone, use the browser's **Add to Home screen** to open DiagTrack with one tap.

Under your name at the top right:

- **My profile:** add your photo, email and mobile number. Alerts use the email and mobile.
- **Compact rows:** fits more tickets on the screen.
- **Change password** and **Sign out**.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page if email has been set up, or ask your
administrator.

## 2. Finding your way

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Go to any screen, or find a ticket | Press `Ctrl K` (or click the search box at the top) and type a ticket number, a word from the title, or a screen name |
| Look at a ticket without leaving a list | Click its row. It opens in a panel on the right. **Open ticket** shows the full page |
| See your alerts | The bell at the top. The red number is how many you have not read |
| Switch between light and dark | The moon or sun button at the top |
| Get the side menu on a phone | The three lines at the top left |

**Pop-ups.** When a ticket that concerns you is raised, closed or escalated, or someone mentions you, a message slides
in at the bottom right within about 15 seconds, with a short sound for new tickets. Click **Open** to go to the
ticket. Turn the sound off in the bell menu. The green **Live** dot at the top means updates are arriving.

## 3. Raising a ticket

1. Press **Raise a ticket**.
2. Choose your **centre** and **what it is about**. A blue line shows which team it goes to.
3. If it is about a machine, choose the machine. Tick **It is not working at all right now** if it is down; this
   records the downtime.
4. Answer any extra questions that appear, such as an error code.
5. Write a short title and what happened: what you saw, when it started, what you already tried.
6. Tick **Patient services are stopped because of this** only when patients cannot be served. The ticket becomes P1
   Critical and the managers are alerted at once.
7. Add photos or files if they help. On a phone, the camera opens.
8. Press **Raise ticket**. You get a number such as KKP-000123 and are taken to the ticket.

**From a machine's QR label:** scan the label with your phone camera. Raise a ticket opens with the centre and machine
already chosen; check the category and describe the fault.

## 4. Following your ticket

**My tickets** lists every ticket you raised.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| New | Raised, waiting for someone to be assigned |
| Assigned | An agent has it but has not started |
| In progress | The agent is working on it |
| On hold | Waiting for you, a vendor or parts. The deadline clock is paused |
| Resolved | The team says it is fixed. Please confirm |
| Closed | Confirmed, or closed by itself 3 days after it was resolved |
| Cancelled | No longer needed |

The bar under each ticket shows the time left: green, amber once 75% of the time is used, red when late.

| You want to | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Add information | Write a comment on the ticket page |
| Say it is urgent | The settings button next to the status, **Raise priority**. Once, with a reason |
| Confirm it is fixed | Open it, click the status button, **Confirm it is fixed**, and give a star rating if you like |
| Say it is not fixed | Status button, **Reopen**, and say what is still wrong. Possible for 7 days after it was resolved |
| Withdraw it | Status button, **Cancel ticket**, until someone starts work |

## 5. The ticket page

The ticket page is laid out like Jira.

- **Title area:** number, priority, title, and quick buttons such as **Attach** and **Assign to me**.
- **Status button** (top right): shows the current status. Click it to move the ticket on, for example to
  **On hold** or **Resolved**. Only the steps you are allowed to take are listed.
- **Settings button** next to it: **Assign**, **Transfer to another department**, **Change priority**.
- **Service level:** the time left to resolve, the due time, and whether the first response was in time.
- **Details:** owner, who raised it, priority, centre, department, category, machine and downtime.
- **Activity:** everything that happened. **All** shows comments and history together; **Comments** and **History**
  show one kind. **Newest first** turns the order round.

**Writing a comment.** Click "Add a comment" or press `M`.

| To get | Type, or use the toolbar |
| --- | --- |
| **Bold** | `**words**` or `Ctrl B` |
| _Italics_ | `_words_` or `Ctrl I` |
| A bulleted list | Start lines with `- ` |
| A numbered list | Start lines with `1. ` |
| A link | The link button, or paste the address |
| Code or an error message | Put it between backticks: `` `E-204` `` |
| Mention someone | Type `@` and the first letters of their name, then pick them. They are alerted |

`Ctrl Enter` saves. Use the paperclip, or drag files onto the box, to attach photos.

**Internal notes** (agents, heads and managers): tick "Internal note" before saving. The requester does not see it;
it shows with an amber edge.

**Editing:** you can correct your own comment for 24 hours. It is then marked "Edited".

**Photos:** click a photo to see it full screen; use the arrows to move between photos.

## 6. For agents

1. **Assigned to me** is your work list, the nearest deadline first. **Team queue** shows the whole department.
2. Open a ticket and choose **Start work** from the status button. This counts as the first response, so do it
   promptly. If you do not start within the response time, the ticket moves to your department head.
3. Reply to the requester with comments. Use internal notes for the team.
4. **Put on hold** when you are waiting for the requester, a vendor or parts, and say why. The clock pauses. Choose
   **Resume work** when you continue.
5. **Transfer** if it belongs to another department, with a reason. That department gets a fresh deadline.
6. **Resolve** with a short note of what was done. For a machine that was down, enter when it started working again.

**Equipment tickets:** press **Log vendor call** when you call the service company (vendor, time, their complaint
number), and **Engineer arrived** when the engineer comes. This measures the vendor's response time.

**Board:** the same tickets as cards in four columns: To do, In progress, On hold, Resolved. Drag a card to the next
column to start, hold or resolve it. The coloured edge shows the priority.

**Assign to me:** on an unassigned ticket of your department, press **Assign to me** to take it.

## 7. For department heads

- **Home** shows tickets that need assigning and those closest to the deadline.
- On a ticket: **Assign** or **Reassign** to an agent, **Change priority** (with a reason), **Cancel ticket** (with a
  reason). You can also take any ticket with **Assign to me**.
- You are alerted when a deadline is close, when one is missed, for every P1, and when an agent does not start in
  time.

## 8. For centre managers, area managers and management

| Screen | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| All tickets | Find any ticket at your centres. Filter by status, priority, centre, department or deadline. **Export to Excel** saves the current list |
| Dashboard | Open tickets by centre, department, status, priority and age; raised and resolved each day; share resolved in time |
| Centres | Every centre side by side: open, P1, late, machines down, raised, resolved, resolved in time, responded in time, average time to resolve, rating and uptime. Green is on target (90% or more), amber is watch (75 to 89%), red is below 75%. Click a number to open those tickets. **Excel** downloads the same figures |
| Reports | Figures by centre, department and more, as Excel or PDF. Section 9 |
| Equipment | Machines and uptime, repeat faults, vendor response times, AMC renewals |

Area managers are alerted on every new ticket at their centres. Management logins can see everything but cannot change
tickets.

## 9. Reports

1. Open **Reports** in the side menu.
2. Choose the **period**: last 7, 30 or 90 days, this month, last month, or **Choose dates**.
3. Choose **Group by**: centre, department, centre and department, category, priority, owner or equipment.
4. Optionally limit the **tickets** to still open or finished.
5. Tick the **centres**, **departments** and **priorities** you want. None ticked means all.
6. Press **Show report**.

The screen shows the totals, a row for each group, a centre by department grid, and the tickets that missed a
deadline. Then:

- **Download Excel:** a workbook with six sheets: Summary, Centre x Department, Tickets (every ticket), Missed
  deadlines, Equipment downtime and Vendor calls. Headers stay in place when scrolling, every column can be filtered,
  and the "in time" columns are coloured green, amber or red.
- **Print or save as PDF:** a page with the Pramodini logo, laid out for A4 landscape. In the print window, choose
  your printer, or "Save as PDF" to make a PDF.

All report figures are about tickets **raised** in the chosen period, and you only see centres and departments you
cover.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Raised | Tickets raised in the period |
| Resolved | Of those, how many are resolved or closed |
| Still open | Of those, how many are still open |
| Past deadline | Missed the resolution deadline, or open and already late |
| Resolved in time | Share of resolved tickets that met the resolution deadline |
| Responded in time | Share of tickets whose first response met the response deadline |
| Average to resolve | Hours from raised to resolved, not counting time on hold |
| Rating | Average of the requesters' star ratings |
