# User guides

Print the part that applies to each person.

## For everyone: raising a ticket

1. Open DiagTrack in the browser and sign in. On a phone, use the browser's "Add to Home screen" for one-tap access.
2. Press **Raise a ticket**.
3. Choose your **centre** and **what it is about**. A blue line shows which team it goes to.
4. For equipment, choose the machine. Tick **It is not working at all right now** if it is down.
5. Write a short title and what happened. Add photos if they help; on a phone the camera opens.
6. Tick **Patient services are stopped** only when patients cannot be served. This marks it P1 Critical and alerts the managers.
7. Press **Raise ticket**. You get a number such as KKP-000123.

**Following your ticket**

- **My tickets** lists everything you raised. The bar shows the time left to the deadline: green, amber after 75%, red when late.
- The **bell** shows updates. Reply on the ticket page to add information.
- **Raise priority** can be used once, with a reason.
- When the ticket is **Resolved**, open it and press **Confirm it is fixed** (you can give a star rating), or **Reopen** and say what is still wrong. If you do nothing it closes after 3 days. You can reopen for 7 days after it was resolved.
- **Cancel ticket** is available until someone starts work.

## For agents

1. **Assigned to me** is your work list, most urgent deadline first. **Team queue** shows the whole department.
2. Open a ticket and press **Start work**. This counts as the first response, so do it promptly.
3. Use **Reply** to talk to the requester. Tick **Internal note** for notes only the team should see.
4. **Hold** when you are waiting for the requester, a vendor or parts. The clock pauses. Press **Resume work** when you continue.
5. **Transfer** if it belongs to another department. Give a reason.
6. **Resolve** with a short note of what was done. For equipment that was down, enter when it started working again.

The **Board** shows the same tickets as cards. Drag a card to *In progress*, *On hold* or *Resolved* to do the same actions.

If you do not start an assigned ticket within its response time, it moves to your department head.

## For department heads

- **Home** shows tickets that need assigning, and those closest to the deadline.
- On a ticket: **Assign** or **Reassign** to an agent, **Change priority** (with a reason), **Cancel ticket** (with a reason).
- **Dashboard**: open load by centre, status, priority and age, and how many were resolved in time. Click any bar to open those tickets.
- You are alerted when a deadline is close, when one is missed, and for every P1.

## For centre and area managers, and management

- **All tickets** shows every ticket of your centres. Filter by status, priority, department or deadline. **Export to Excel** saves the current list.
- **Dashboard** and **Equipment** show performance and machine uptime.
- Area managers are alerted on every new ticket in their centres (this can be switched off in Settings).
- Management logins can view everything but not change tickets.

## Shortcuts

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | Jump to search. Type a ticket number to open it directly |
| `n` | Raise a new ticket |
| Moon or sun button | Switch between light and dark |
